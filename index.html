Meet your dermatologist

Dr Uma Alagappan

A consultant dermatologist with interests in paediatric, women’s and general dermatology, including chronic eczema, food allergy and immunodermatology.

Her background includes dermatology training at Changi General Hospital and the National Skin Centre, followed by paediatric dermatology experience at KK Women’s and Children’s Hospital.

Does the clinic see adults and children?

Yes. The clinic provides adult and paediatric dermatology. Dr Uma’s interests include children’s skin, women’s dermatology, chronic eczema, food allergy and immunodermatology.

Where is the Paragon clinic?

The clinic is at 290 Orchard Road, #09-20/21 Paragon Medical Centre, Singapore 238859. Call 6320 0310 to confirm your visit.

Which number should I use?

Call Parkway East on 6517 9760 or Paragon on 6320 0310. The shared WhatsApp-only number is 8205 5209. Include your preferred clinic in your message.

Is my appointment confirmed when I message?

No. Please wait for the clinic team to confirm your appointment date, time and location.
