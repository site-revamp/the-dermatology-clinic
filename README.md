# The Dermatology Clinic — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

The Dermatology Clinic provides specialist skin care for adults and children at Paragon and Parkway East in Singapore. Its services include medical, paediatric, cosmetic and laser dermatology, with Dr Uma Alagappan publicly identified as its consultant dermatologist.

The redesigned homepage helps patients discover care sooner, follow valid treatment links and find consistent clinic information before visiting.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Valid, Descriptive Treatment Links

Clearly labelled links to relevant existing pages replace incomplete “Know More” links. Previously, several homepage buttons did not lead to valid treatment pages. Patients can now open the intended care information directly.

### 2. A Consistent Paragon Address

The Paragon unit number now appears consistently as #09-20/21, matching the main contact details and Paragon Medical’s directory. This resolves conflicting references to #09-01/02 and #09-20/21, reducing uncertainty when planning a visit.

### 3. Earlier Care Pathways

Six care pathways appear earlier, alongside a condensed introduction to Dr Uma and a link to her full profile. Previously, repeated biography and training information delayed service discovery. The revised order brings care options forward while retaining access to her detailed background.

## Design Improvements

- **Layout and hierarchy:** Earlier care pathways and a shorter introduction prioritise patient needs.
- **Navigation clarity:** Descriptive treatment links identify their destinations.
- **Consistency:** Paragon’s unit number follows one supported address throughout the homepage.
- **Content presentation:** Reduced biography repetition creates a clearer progression towards care options.
